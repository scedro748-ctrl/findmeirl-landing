4. Our Services

The App provides a privacy-first platform for social discovery and venue engagement. Key features include:

  • Discover: Interactive map displaying obfuscated positions for Personal Users and fixed public pins for Venues, together with a horizontal people strip and followed-venue posts strip. Users may choose whether their obfuscated location appears as a map pin.
  • Events: Creation, discovery, and RSVP functionality with automatic group chat creation for each event
  • Chats: Direct messaging between connected Users and event-linked group chats (with host administrator privileges, member management, and ban functionality), with optional AI assistance for message suggestions
  • Connections & Contacts: Sending, accepting, declining, and blocking connection requests; viewing accepted contacts
  • Profile: Personal profile management, interest selection, soulmate matching scores, gallery, privacy controls (including private mode and online status opt-out), and optional AI-assisted profile optimization suggestions
  • Business & Venues: Business account signup, venue listing editor (including address, coordinates, opening hours), post composition and timeline management, follower insights, and public discoverability for approved Venues

We use grid-based location obfuscation so that exact GPS coordinates of Personal Users are never shown to others.
