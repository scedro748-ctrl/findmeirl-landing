Privacy Policy

findmeIRL (“Findme”, “we”, “us”, or “our”)

1. Introduction

Welcome to findmeIRL, a privacy-first social discovery mobile application and related services (collectively, the “App” or “Services”). The App enables users to discover nearby individuals with shared interests while protecting exact location through grid-based obfuscation, and allows registered businesses and venues to create public profiles, post updates, and engage with followers.

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use the App. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service. Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings set forth in the Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect the following information directly from you:

Personal Users
  • Account credentials: email address and password
  • Profile information: nickname, birthdate, gender, country(ies) of origin, current country of residence, profession, faith, languages spoken, interests (predefined and custom), bio, avatar, banner image, and optional gallery photos (up to 15)
Business Users (Venue Owners)
  • Business profile details: business name, contact person, business email, phone number, description, category, physical address, website URL, avatar/logo, banner image, owner-selected geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude), opening hours schedule (JSON format), and moderation status fields (paused_at, paused_until, paused_reason_internal)
  • Business content: posts including title, body, post type (promotion, update, event, behind-the-scenes, testimonial, community), optional image or external link
User-Generated Content (All Users)
  • Events created or RSVPed to, chat messages, connection requests, reports, and reactions

2.2 Location Data

Personal Profiles: We collect precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) only when you actively enable location sharing. These coordinates are stored server-side solely to compute a grid cell identifier (approximately 1 km²) and generate an obfuscated position (randomized offset of approximately 500–800 meters). Your exact GPS coordinates are never displayed to other users.

Business Venues: Venue locations are selected by the Business User and are intentionally public and non-obfuscated. These fixed coordinates are used to display venue pins on the Discover map and are visible to all App users. Business Users may update these coordinates at any time through the App.

Automatically Derived: Grid cell identifiers for proximity matching.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information: model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token for push notification delivery
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g., feature usage, session duration)
  • IP address and coarse location (country level) for analytics and abuse prevention
  • Geocoding requests via third-party services (e.g., Nominatim) when a Business User searches for or sets a venue address

2.4 Interest and Matching Data

We collect and process your selected interests and custom interest submissions to power personalized recommendations, soulmate matching scores, and Discover feed ranking. Custom interest submissions are subject to administrative review before being added to the public catalog. Match scores are computed server-side and shared only in aggregated or anonymized form within the App. Optional AI assistance features (profile optimization and chat suggestions) process your profile and conversation context transiently to generate personalized suggestions; inputs are not stored for model training or retained beyond the immediate request.

2.5 No Third-Party Advertising or Data Brokerage

We do not integrate advertising SDKs, sell personal data to data brokers, or use your information for behavioral advertising.

2.6 User-Generated Content and Image Scanning

All images uploaded to the App (avatars, banners, gallery photos, event covers/memories, business logos, banners, and posts) are first placed in a private staging bucket (temp-uploads) and scanned by an automated AI system (Google Cloud Vision Safe Search) before being promoted to public storage. The scan detects illegal, explicit, or harmful content. Content that fails the scan is blocked from publication.

User-submitted text—such as profile biographies, business descriptions, custom hobby or interest submissions, and business posts—is processed by an automated AI content moderation system. This system analyzes text for prohibited categories including hate speech, harassment, threats, explicit or sexual content, violence, spam, scams, and other violations. Content that exceeds defined risk thresholds may be blocked from publication or flagged for review. This helps maintain a safe environment for all users.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate core App features, including map-based discovery (people and venues), interest-based matching, real-time chat, events, connections, and business post timelines
  • Calculate obfuscated locations and grid-based proximity for Personal Users while displaying fixed public coordinates for Venues
  • Personalize the user experience through filters, recommendations, trending tags, and city-specific interest insights
  • Deliver push notifications (only with your explicit permission) for messages, connection activity, event updates, and business posts from followed venues
  • Enable Business Users to manage listings, publish posts, view follower counts and insights, and moderate their venue content
  • Enforce safety, moderation, and compliance (including in-app reporting, blocking, chat bans, administrative review of custom interests, proactive AI moderation of bios / descriptions / posts / submissions for hate speech and other prohibited content, and automated AI triage of reports)
  • Record your acceptance of these Terms and this Policy (via the terms_accepted_at timestamp on your profile) for compliance and audit purposes
  • Improve, debug, and analyze App performance using anonymized or aggregated data
  • Power optional AI assistance features that generate profile and chat suggestions using secure, privacy-preserving AI processing (no user data is used to train models)
  • Respond to legal requests and protect the rights, safety, and property of Findme, Users, and the public

We use in-App reports and automated tools—including AI text moderation for user-generated content and AI triage of reports—to detect and respond to violations. Repeated reports against the same subject may trigger escalation review. Event creation requires hosts to confirm compliance with our non-discrimination policy (see the Terms of Service section on Open Events and Non-Discrimination for details). This attestation is recorded and helps maintain an inclusive environment.

4. How We Share (or Do Not Share) Your Information

We never:
  • ✕ Share your exact GPS coordinates with any other User
  • ✕ Sell or rent your personal data
  • ✕ Share data with third parties for advertising or data brokerage purposes

We share information in the following limited circumstances:

With other Users

Obfuscated location and profile data for Personal Users (subject to your privacy settings, connection status, and is_private flag); fixed public venue coordinates, business profile details, and approved business posts for Venues. Business posts are visible to followers and, for approved venues, publicly via API.

With service providers

Data is shared with contracted processors bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage, realtime subscriptions, Row-Level Security
  • Firebase — push notification delivery via FCM
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap contributors — geocoding for venue address lookup (no persistent storage of queries by Findme)
Legal and safety

When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Findme, Users, or the public.

Business transfers

In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to affected Users where required).

5. Location Data – Special Notice for Personal Users

Precise location access is essential for the core discovery functionality of the App. You control when location is shared via device settings (“When In Use” permission). Exact coordinates remain on our servers and are used exclusively for grid computation and obfuscation. Other Users see only a randomized position within the same ~1 km² grid cell. You may stop sharing location at any time. You can also choose whether your obfuscated location appears as a map pin via the show_avatar_on_map setting. Business venue coordinates are owner-controlled and publicly visible by design.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide the Services and comply with legal obligations. Upon account deletion (initiated by you in Profile → Account or by us for violations), we perform cascading deletion of your profile, messages, connections, events, business profiles (if owned), posts, follows, and gallery photos, subject to any legal hold or backup retention requirements. Location history for Personal Users is retained only for a limited period. Anonymized or aggregated data (including match score statistics and usage analytics) may be retained indefinitely for service improvement.

Account suspension (via suspended_at / suspended_until) or Business Venue pause (via paused_at / paused_until) temporarily restricts access or visibility while retaining the underlying data. Full deletion only occurs on explicit user request or permanent termination. Suspended/paused records remain subject to the same retention rules until the account is fully deleted.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, rectify, or erase your personal data
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You may exercise these rights directly in the App (Profile → Account section for deletion and certain edits) or by contacting us at the address below. We respond to valid requests within 30 days (or sooner for deletion requests). Business Users may additionally manage venue visibility, follower lists, and post content through the App. If your account or venue has been suspended or paused for policy violations, you may appeal the decision through the in-App support flow or by contacting us.

Additional controls available in the App:
  • Hide individual conversations from your inbox until new activity occurs
  • Opt out of showing online presence to peers
  • Control whether your avatar appears on the map
  • Set your profile as private (minimal card visible to non-connections)

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including Row-Level Security (RLS) policies in our database, encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where applicable, strict access controls, scoped API keys, and regular security reviews. Automated pre-publication scanning of user-uploaded images (via Google Cloud Vision Safe Search on the private temp-uploads bucket) helps prevent the distribution of illegal or harmful content. No system is 100% secure. We strongly encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable any available two-factor authentication.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended exclusively for individuals aged 16 years and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal data from anyone under the age of 16. If we become aware that a User is under 16, we will promptly delete the account and all associated data. Business accounts are subject to the same age restriction. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided data should contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Our primary infrastructure provider (Supabase) processes data in secure data centers located primarily in the European Economic Area. When personal data is transferred outside your country of residence, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including standard contractual clauses and compliance with applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection).
